## 🎨 DESIGN ELEMENTS

### Text Elements in All Templates:
1. **Company Name** - Large, bold, prominent
2. **Tagline** - Descriptive, smaller text
3. **Name** - Medium-sized, bold
4. **Position/Title** - Smaller, regular weight
5. **Contact Info** - Email and phone number

### Shape Elements Used:
1. **Rectangles** - Backgrounds, accent bars, decorative blocks
2. **Circles** - Decorative accents, logo placeholders
3. **Lines** - Accent lines, dividers
4. **Gradients** - Simulated with overlapping shapes

### Color Schemes:
- **Professional**: Navy, blue, gold, silver, white
- **Minimal**: Black, white, gray
- **Creative**: Gradients, vibrant colors, pink, purple
- **Luxury**: Black, gold, white
- **Tech**: Dark blue, cyan, neon accents
- **Medical**: White, blue, clean
- **Real Estate**: White, gold, elegant
